1. Project Overview


PrepBite is a food delivery website that allows users to browse restaurants, explore menus, add items to a cart, and place food orders. The project started as an HTML/CSS frontend and is being fully migrated to React.js and Tailwind CSS to make it component-based, responsive, and production-ready.


1.1 What PrepBite Does
Users land on a homepage showcasing featured restaurants and popular dishes
Users can browse all restaurants filtered by cuisine, rating, or delivery time
Users can open a restaurant page and view its full menu with categories
Users can add items to a cart, adjust quantities, and see a live order summary
Users can proceed to a checkout page and review their order before placing it
Phase 2 (future): user auth, real order placement, payment gateway, order tracking
